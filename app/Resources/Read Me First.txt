Printer Studio KH — Read Me First
==============================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
A menu bar app that keeps this Mac's shared printers reachable from other
Macs and iOS devices — and that can set up an HP Laser 107a, a USB-only
printer Apple has no driver for, so every device on your network can print
to it.

The app lives in the menu bar as a small printer icon. Click it to open
Printer Studio KH, to fix shared queues, or to quit.


OPENING IT THE FIRST TIME — PLEASE READ
---------------------------------------
This app is not sold through the App Store. The first time you open it on
a Mac that received it over AirDrop, Telegram, email or a download, macOS
will refuse, saying the developer "cannot be verified", or that the app is
damaged and should be moved to the Trash.

That is macOS being cautious about anything arriving over a network. The
app is fine. To open it:

    1. Find "Printer Studio KH" in your Applications folder
    2. Right-click it (or hold Control and click), and choose "Open"
    3. Click "Open" again in the dialog that appears

You only do this once per Mac. After that it opens normally.

If macOS instead offers "Open Anyway" under
System Settings > Privacy & Security, that button does the same thing.

Why this is necessary: Apple only removes that warning for apps signed
with a paid Apple Developer account and notarized by Apple. Without that,
one right-click is the price. There is no way around it from inside the
app, because the warning appears before the app is allowed to run.


AFTER IT OPENS
--------------
Nothing else needs doing by hand. The app can:

  * show every printer on this Mac, and whether it is shared
  * keep shared queues open to the network — the fix for "Job held for
    authentication" on other Macs — either on demand or automatically in
    the background
  * install the HP Laser 107a driver, create its print queue and share it.
    macOS will ask for your password once, because installing a printer
    driver needs administrator rights.

The driver is bundled inside this app. There is nothing else to copy.


SENDING THIS APP TO ANOTHER MAC
-------------------------------
Send the app on its own. It carries this file inside it, so there is no
separate Read Me to send.

On the receiving Mac, do the right-click > Open step above, once.

After that the driver install works normally. The app deliberately clears
the "downloaded from the internet" flag from the driver files as it
installs them — without that step, macOS would quietly refuse to run the
newly installed driver, and print jobs would sit there doing nothing. That
is handled for you.


TROUBLE?
--------
Open the app and look at the Activity tab. It says in plain English what
it did, and what went wrong if something did.

For a fuller picture you can also run the diagnostic report that ships
alongside this app in the project folder:

    ./app/report.sh && ./dist/printer-studio-report
